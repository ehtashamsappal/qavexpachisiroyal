♛ THE ANCIENT GAME OF KINGS, REIMAGINED ♛

Pachisi — the 1,500-year-old royal Indian race game that inspired Ludo — returns
in its most majestic edition yet. Command four armies, cast the cowrie dice,
capture your rivals, build blockades, and race all four pieces home to claim
the crown.

🎲 OFFICIAL CLASSIC RULES
• Authentic Pachisi: 52-square royal circuit, 8 star-marked safe castles,
  blockades, captures, extra turns, and exact-roll home finishes
• Digital cowrie dice (2d6) with the beloved doubles & six traditions
• Three consecutive doubles forfeits the turn — fortune punishes greed!

⚔️ 6 GAME MODES
• Classic Royal — the full 4-player royal court
• Quick Match — a swift duel against the AI
• Vs AI — face 1–3 AI courtiers, Easy to Expert
• Pass & Play — 2–4 humans, one device
• Timed Blitz — 5 minutes on the clock, most progress wins
• Tournament Ladder — climb 5 rungs from Easy to Expert

🤖 4 AI DIFFICULTIES
Time-capped tactical AI: Easy plays for fun, Medium punishes mistakes,
Hard looks ahead, and Expert plans like a true maharaja.

🎨 8 ROYAL THEMES
Maharaja Court (signature royal red & gold), Midnight, Ocean, Neon, Desert,
Forest, Mono Ink, Sunset — each with a unique 6-color palette, unlockable
with play-earned Royal Coins.

🏆 52 ACHIEVEMENTS • DAILY STREAKS • ROYAL COINS
Earn coins by playing, unlock dice skins and court frames, keep your daily
streak alive, and fill the Hall of Fame.

👨‍👩‍👧 FAMILY FIRST
• Kids Mode: capture-free, blockade-free racing for young players
• Accessibility: larger text, high contrast, reduce motion, haptics control
• 100% OFFLINE — no account, no ads, no tracking, ever

☕ ONE OPTIONAL TIP

HOW TO PLAY
Roll a 6 to march a piece out of your yard. Race around the cross-shaped
board, capture lone rivals (they return to start!), shelter on star castles,
stack pairs into blockades, and bring all four pieces home with exact rolls.
