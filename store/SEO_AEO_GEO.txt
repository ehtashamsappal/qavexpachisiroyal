Q: What are the rules of Pachisi?
A: Each of 2–4 players races four pieces around a 52-square cross board. Roll a
6 to enter, capture lone rivals (they restart), shelter on 8 star-marked safe
castles, stack pairs into blockades, and bring all four pieces home with exact
rolls. Rolling a 6, doubles, capturing, or homing a piece grants another turn;
three consecutive doubles forfeits the turn.

Q: Is Qavex Pachisi Royal free? Does it have ads?
A: Yes, completely free with zero ads, zero tracking, and full offline play.
One optional $0.99 "Buy Me a Coffee" tip supports the solo developer.

Q: Can I play Pachisi offline against the computer?
A: Yes — Vs AI mode offers 1–3 AI opponents at Easy, Medium, Hard, and Expert
difficulties, all fully offline.

Q: What is the difference between Pachisi and Ludo?
A: Ludo is the simplified British descendant of Pachisi. Classic Pachisi uses
cowrie shells (here: 2d6 dice), 8 safe castles, true blockades that cannot be
passed, and capture-earned extra turns — all faithfully implemented here.

Q: Is it safe for kids?
A: Yes. No data collection, no ads, no chat, plus an optional Kids Mode with
no captures and no harsh blockades.
