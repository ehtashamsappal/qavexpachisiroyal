1. OVERVIEW
Qavex Pachisi Royal ("the Game") is an offline board game. This policy
describes what information the Game does — and does not — handle.

2. DATA WE DO NOT COLLECT
The Game collects no personal data whatsoever:
  • No accounts, logins, names, emails, or phone numbers.
  • No device identifiers, advertising IDs, or location data.
  • No analytics, crash reporters, or tracking SDKs.
  • No advertisements of any kind.

3. DATA STORED ON YOUR DEVICE
All game progress — profile name/avatar, Royal Coins, statistics,
achievements, daily streak, settings, themes, and shop ownership — is stored
locally on your device using the platform's private app storage
(SharedPreferences on Android). It never leaves your device and is deleted
if you uninstall the Game.

4. IN-APP PURCHASE
The Game offers exactly one optional in-app purchase: "Buy Me a Coffee"
($0.99, product ID: qavexpachisiroyalcoffee), a voluntary tip for the
developer. The purchase is processed entirely by Google Play; QAVEX receives
no payment details, card numbers, or billing information — only an
anonymised confirmation that a purchase occurred.

5. CHILDREN
The Game is safe for all ages. Because no personal data is collected and
there is no chat, user-generated content, or advertising, the Game is
suitable for children. An optional Kids Mode further softens gameplay
(no captures, no harsh blockades). Parents: all settings are on-device and
under your control.

6. PERMISSIONS
The Game requests no dangerous Android permissions. It functions fully
offline; an internet connection is required only if you choose to make the
optional tip purchase (handled by Google Play).

7. DATA SHARING & SALE
We do not sell, rent, or share any data — because we collect none.

8. YOUR RIGHTS & CONTACT
Since no personal data is processed, there is nothing to access, correct,
or delete on our servers. For any privacy question, contact:
  games@qavex.company | +923375621550 | https://www.qavex.company/

9. CHANGES
If this policy changes, the updated version will ship with the Game and be
published at https://www.qavex.company/.
